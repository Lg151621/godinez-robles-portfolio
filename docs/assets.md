# Original assets

Both images were created with the built-in image-generation tool, copied into `public/images/`, and are not dependencies on a remote asset service. The generated architecture image is illustrative, not a real client project.

## public/images/dawn-sky.png

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-bleed atmospheric hero background for a premium web design portfolio. Create a cinematic fine-art photograph of enormous soft cumulus clouds at dawn, seen from above the cloud layer. Wide landscape 16:9 composition. A soft slate-blue open sky in the upper two thirds, sculptural ivory cloud banks along the lower edge and right side, restrained amber sunlight seeping through the lower left horizon. Delicate film grain, natural atmospheric haze, subtle pale blue and warm cream colors. Expansive calm negative space in the center for oversized cream typography added separately in code. Moody photographic depth, slightly underexposed sky for readability, warm lit cloud edges. No text, logos, typography, objects, planes, buildings, mountains, watermarks, purple tones or synthetic neon gradients.

## public/images/solace-house.png

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial architecture photograph for a clearly labeled portfolio concept website. Wide landscape 16:9. A quiet modernist pale limestone house in a Mediterranean coastal landscape, simple horizontal volumes, a large shaded opening, olive trees and tall dry grass, hazy sea in the distance. Architecture on the right two thirds, open shadowed sky and landscape in the upper left for typography added separately. Beautiful restrained magazine photography, deep olive greens, sandstone, soft ivory, warm late afternoon sunlight, natural grain. Eye-level shot, contemplative composition. No people, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Realistic materials and structure, avoid surreal features.
